A little about me

Hi, I'm Karthik.

I treat every random idea like a side quest. Some turn into working projects, some turn into 40 open browser tabs, and one turned into a system that looks inside lunar craters the sun has never seen. I mostly build with computer vision and ML, because teaching a machine to see still feels a little like cheating.

I came for the code and stayed for the physics. It's almost unfair how well math describes the universe, as if the cosmos left its source code lying around in equation form and we're slowly learning to read it.

Away from the keyboard, I'm usually watching things go very fast: rockets, F1 cars, a tennis ball with so much topspin it changes its mind mid-flight. When I slow down, it's for a game of chess I fully intend to win, a violin or piano piece on repeat, or a good paradox that keeps me thinking well past midnight.
