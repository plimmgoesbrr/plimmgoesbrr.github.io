Spotify Taste Lab

What does your music taste look like as a map? A side idea I've been itching to build: turning your listening history into something you can explore.

// the 10-second version

Your playlists are secretly a map of you. Taste Lab would draw that map, find the friend whose map looks most like yours, and point you to music you'd probably love but haven't found yet.

// the plan

What it would do

  • Taste map: see which moods and genres you actually live in, not the ones you think you do.
  • Music twin: compare with friends to find whose taste overlaps yours most.
  • Taste drift: watch how your listening changes month to month.
  • Edge picks: songs just outside your comfort zone: close enough to like, far enough to surprise you.

// how it would work

The pipeline I have in mind

  1. ConnectLog in with Spotify to read your top artists, top tracks and recent plays.
  2. DescribeTurn each artist into numbers from genres, popularity and listening patterns.
  3. MapSquash those numbers onto a 2D map where similar music sits close together.
  4. Compare and recommendMeasure overlap between people, and look for great music just past the edge of your map.

// the tricky part

Describing a song without hearing it

Spotify has restricted some of its audio-analysis data for new apps, so ready-made "energy" or "danceability" numbers are off the table. The challenge is building a good picture of taste from what's left: genres, artist connections and listening habits.

Still on the drawing board. Ideas, or want to be an early music-twin test subject? Say hi.
